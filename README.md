#🐱 Pet Simulator (React + TypeScript)

A fun interactive pet simulator web app where your virtual pet reacts differently based on how you treat it 👀
Feed it, pet it, annoy it… or ignore it — but be careful, your actions have consequences 😈

##✨ Features:

-🐾 Dynamic mood changes based on user actions
-🍗 Feeding system (yes, you can overfeed!)
-🩷 Petting system with evolving reactions
-😶 Ignore & 😈 Annoy interactions with unique dialogue
-🎭 Multiple mood-based images
-💬 Funny and expressive cat dialogues
-💡 Hidden reactions when buttons are clicked multiple times


##🎮 How to Use:

-Click Feed 🍗 to feed your pet
-Click Pet 🩷 to show affection
-Click Ignore 💔 to… well… ignore it 😬
-Click Annoy 😈 at your own risk
-Try clicking buttons multiple times to unlock different reactions 😉

🛠️ Tech Stack:

-React
-TypeScript
-CSS
